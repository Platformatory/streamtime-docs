Getting Streamtime installed, configured, and running your first
workload. Work down the list:

1. **[Installation]({{ site.baseurl }}/getting-started/installation/)**: the
   single image, Docker Compose, or the Helm chart; interactive or headless.
2. **[First-time setup]({{ site.baseurl }}/getting-started/first-time-setup/)**:
   the setup wizard, step by step.
3. **[Configuration]({{ site.baseurl }}/getting-started/configuration/)**:
   domains and certificates, identity providers, and the cloud accounts
   your fleets will use. Everything you can replace with your own is under
   [Bring your own]({{ site.baseurl }}/bring-your-own/).
4. **[Interfaces]({{ site.baseurl }}/getting-started/interfaces/)**:
   connect the API, SDKs, CLI, MCP and Terraform.
5. **[User Management]({{ site.baseurl }}/getting-started/user-management/)**:
   organizations, members and roles.
6. **[API keys and OAuth clients]({{ site.baseurl }}/getting-started/api-keys/)**:
   credentials for automation.
7. **[Package Management]({{ site.baseurl }}/getting-started/package-management/)**:
   the packages and versions Streamtime deploys, and how upgrades roll out.

Then continue with [Fleet Management]({{ site.baseurl }}/fleet-management/) to create
a fleet and [Workloads]({{ site.baseurl }}/workloads/) to run something on it.
